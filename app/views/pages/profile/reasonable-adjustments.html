NHS reasonable adjustments are small changes that help you to get the healthcare you need.

You may be able to get them if you have a disability or another need that makes accessing healthcare harder for you.

All health and care services must be as accessible to people with disabilities as they are for everyone else. This is stated in the Equality Act 2010
