To check notes from your GP surgery appointments:

  1. Select Appointments on the home screen of the NHS App.
  2. Select Appointment notes and other updates.

This will show any appointment notes your GP surgery has shared. It may also include other messages or updates from your GP surgery.

If information is missing or incorrect

The information in this area of the app comes from your GP surgery.

Contact your GP surgery if:

  • you cannot see this information
  • information is missing or incorrect
  • you can see information that should not be there (for example, you can see information that is not yours)
