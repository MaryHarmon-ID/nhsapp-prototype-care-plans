To book a GP surgery appointment:

  1. Select Appointments on the home screen of the NHS App.
  2. Select Book a GP surgery appointment.

This leads to two options:

Ask about a health problem

Select Ask about a health problem to complete an online form describing your symptoms. Your GP surgery will then contact you to offer an appointment or advice.

Check for available appointments

Alternatively, select Check for available appointments to see whether your GP surgery has made any appointments directly available to book through the app.

If your GP surgery does not use an online form,“Check for available appointments” will be the only booking option showing in your appointments area.
