The NHS App shows details of your one-off and repeat medicines. This may include medicines you are currently taking, as well as ones you had in the past.

To view your medicines record:

  1. Select Prescriptions on the home screen.
  2. Select Medicines record.

If information is missing or incorrect

The information in this part of the app comes from your GP surgery.

Contact your GP surgery if:

  • you cannot see this information
  • information is missing or incorrect
  • you can see information that should not be there (for example, you can see information that is not yours)
